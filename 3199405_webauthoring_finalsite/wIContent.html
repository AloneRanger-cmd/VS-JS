Why Barkin Bayoğlu is My Hero

I chose Barkin Bayoğlu as my hero because he represents responsibility, integrity, and positive influence. Unlike many figures in motorcycle media, Barkin never promoted reckless behavior or unsafe riding. He used his platform to educate others, often emphasizing that riding is a serious responsibility rather than a form of entertainment. His videos often included safety tips and encouraged riders to respect traffic laws. Beyond his advocacy for safe riding, Barkin was an inspirational figure to many motorcycle riders in Turkey. Barkin is a hero because of the lasting positive impact he had on thousands of Turkish riders including me.
