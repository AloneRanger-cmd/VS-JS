Life of Barkin Bayoglu

Barkin Bayoğlu was born in Istanbul in 1975. Known as a Turkish YouTuber and motorcycle racer, he began his primary education in Istanbul but continued it in England due to his family's emigration. He studied political science in Belgium and, upon returning to Türkiye, completed his studies in Political Science and International Relations at Bilgi University.
His motorcycle riding videos gained popularity, leading him to appear on Showmax with the program "Piston." After 10 episodes, he created the program "Altin Elbiseli Adam" (The Man in the Golden Suit) on Skytürk. Following this television program, in 2010, he launched a YouTube channel called "Altin Elbiseli Adam," focusing on motorcycles. On his YouTube channel, he reviewed various motorcycle brands and models, and provided information about motorsports, equipment, and riding techniques. His videos have garnered over 110 million views.
He died on July 25, 2017, in a traffic accident at the exit of the 15 July Martyrs Bridge and was buried in Çengelköy Cemetery. According to the accident report released as a result of the investigation, it was determined that he was not at fault.
